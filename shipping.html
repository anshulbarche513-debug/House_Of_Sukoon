Clear, simple information for receiving your House of Sukoon order.

01

Shipping

Complimentary shipping applies to orders above ₹1,999. Delivery timelines and courier details are confirmed with your order.

02

Order support

For questions about an order, contact support@houseofsukoon.com or WhatsApp +91 88714 93221.

03

Returns

For hygiene and safety, fragrance returns should be handled according to the final store policy configured before launch. Contact us first so the team can guide you.

04

Damaged delivery

If a parcel arrives damaged, photograph the package and contact the House promptly with your order details.
